# Remaining raid final bosses

Checked 2026-11-18 against the [raid list](https://www.destinypedia.com/Raid), including vaulted Destiny 2 raids, raid lairs and reprised Destiny 1 raids. Recommendations below are editorial judgments about recognizability and useful clues. Following user approval, all six Destiny 2 candidates below have been added for playtesting. Outstanding clue choices are tracked in [review.md](review.md). Aksis remains excluded.

## Destiny 2 candidates

| Final boss | Raid or raid lair | Evaluation | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Argos, Planetary Core](https://www.destinypedia.com/Argos,_Planetary_Core) | Leviathan, Eater of Worlds | Distinctive Vex Hydra tied to the Leviathan. Recognizable to players of the removed raid lair, but less developed as a character; potential clue overlap with other Vex bosses. | Optional; useful for complete raid coverage, lower priority for a story-focused roster. |
| [Val Ca'uor](https://www.destinypedia.com/Val_Ca%27uor) | Spire of Stars | Red Legion leader with a recognizable raid encounter and a later Taken incarnation. Would need a clear choice between Cabal raid identity and later form. | Include; use the recognizable original raid incarnation and retain the later form in research notes. |
| [INSURRECTION PRIME, Kell's Scourge](https://www.destinypedia.com/INSURRECTION_PRIME,_Kell%27s_Scourge) | Scourge of the Past | Memorable Servitor-powered mech and a useful Kell's Scourge affiliation. Its being type needs an explicit gameplay choice, rather than conflating the Servitor core with an ordinary Eliksni body. | Include after deciding its being-type label. |
| [Gahlran, the Sorrow-Bearer](https://www.destinypedia.com/Gahlran,_the_Sorrow-Bearer) | Crown of Sorrow | Strong story connection to Calus, the Crown and Savathûn. A later Nightmare appearance adds recognizability without needing a second character entry. | Include; use the original Cabal incarnation. |
| [Sanctified Mind, Sol Inherent](https://www.destinypedia.com/Sanctified_Mind,_Sol_Inherent) | Garden of Salvation | Recognizable final boss with a clear Sol Divisive affiliation. Weaker individual characterization; likely shares several clues with other Vex Minds. | Include for raid coverage, but lower priority if prioritizing developed story characters. |
| [Koregos, the Worldline](https://www.destinypedia.com/Koregos,_the_Worldline) | The Desert Perpetual | Distinctive final encounter tied to the Nessian Schism and the Nine's realm. The Epic form, Koregos, Fractured in Time, should be an alias/form of the same character rather than a duplicate. | Include as one entry covering both forms. |

Koregos is the final boss, not Epoptes, Agraios or Iatros. The [raid encounter guide](https://www.destinypedia.com/The_Desert_Perpetual) documents both normal and Epic versions. Some encounters can be selected in a different order after a clear; this does not create additional final-boss identities for the roster.

## Already represented

Emperor Calus (Leviathan), Riven (Last Wish), Taniks (Deep Stone Crypt), Atheon (Vault of Glass), Rhulk (Vow of the Disciple), Oryx (King's Fall), Nezarec (Root of Nightmares), Crota (Crota's End), and the Witness (Salvation's Edge) are already playable after this expansion. Their forms do not need separate entries. Other encounter bosses and dungeon bosses are outside this final-boss audit.
