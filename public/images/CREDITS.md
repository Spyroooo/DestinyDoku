# Image sources

Weapon thumbnails and the Strand damage icon are Bungie Destiny 2 assets, obtained
from the public Destiny Manifest and bungie.net content paths on 2026-11-09.
Destiny and its imagery belong to Bungie. This is an unofficial fan project.

Other category SVGs are from justrealmilk/destiny-icons:
https://github.com/justrealmilk/destiny-icons
The repository's CC0 license is retained in categories/LICENSE.

Linear fusion rifles currently share the fusion-rifle silhouette. Source, slot,
rarity, and release icons are illustrative category symbols; the accompanying
text provides the precise value.

All images are served locally; gameplay does not call the Bungie API.
